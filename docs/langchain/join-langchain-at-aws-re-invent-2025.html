If you're hitting challenges with production agents or trying to figure out evaluation strategies, this is a good place to get technical feedback.

We're participating in a few technical sessions during the week:

LangChain x OpenSearch Event

December 1st | 5:30-8:30pm PT | Encore Hotel

Our engineering team will cover context engineering patterns we've seen work well with LangGraph and OpenSearch. There will be live demos, a panel discussion, and time to connect with other developers. This event is open to all attendees. Register now.

Redis Partner Lightning Session

December 3rd at 1:15pm | Redis Booth, Expo Floor - Booth #1520

Our engineering team will co-present with Redis a 10-minute lightning talk on the basics of context engineering and demo a deep-research AI agent built with LangChain and Redis.

AI LIVE at re:Invent with LangChain

December 2nd at 12:33-12:53 PM PT | The Venetian Expo Floor – Level 2, Hall B, Partner Experience Pavilion #884 | Booth - Partners LIVE! #1190

Hear from our team live on the expo floor! We’ll be discussing the evolution from simple LLM applications to full-fledged agentic systems — covering context engineering, deep agents, and the agent development lifecycle.
