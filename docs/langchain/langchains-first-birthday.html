📌 Some Favorite Papers

Thanks for advancing our thinking about Generative AI.

Thanks for advancing our thinking about Generative AI.

  • ReAct: For being one of the first papers to tackle the problem of how to get LLMs to improve their action taking ability

authors: Shunyu Yao, Jeffrey Zhao, Dian Yu, Nan Du, Izhak Shafran, Karthik Narasimhan, Yuan Cao

  • RestGPT: For exploring how to connect LLMs to arbitrary REST APIs

authors: Yifan Song, Weimin Xiong, Dawei Zhu, Wenhao Wu, Han Qian, Mingbo Song, Hailiang Huang, Cheng Li, Ke Wang, Rong Yao, Ye Tian, Sujian Li

  • Generative Agents: For introducing novel memory techniques

authors: Joon Sung Park, Joseph C. O'Brien, Carrie J. Cai, Meredith Ringel Morris, Percy Liang, Michael S. Bernstein

  • Plan-and-Solve: For adding an explicit planning step to structure future LLM interactions

authors: Lei Wang, Wanyu Xu, Yihuai Lan, Zhiqiang Hu, Yunshi Lan, Roy Ka-Wei Lee, Ee-Peng Lim

  • CoALA: For helping us organize how we think about agents.

authors: Theodore R. Sumers, Shunyu Yao, Karthik Narasimhan, Thomas L. Griffiths
