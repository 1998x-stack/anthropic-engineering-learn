📖 What is Lyzr?

Lyzr is a full-stack agent framework that specializes in building fully autonomous AI agents for enterprises. Their focus is on achieving Organizational General Intelligence (OGI) by harnessing agent data. Lyzr offers pre-built agents like Jazon, an AI sales development representative, and Skott, an AI content marketing agent, as well as a no-code builder for custom agent creation. Their platform enables organizations to build, deploy, and manage AI agents that can handle complex tasks and workflows autonomously.

📚 How does LlamaIndex help?

LlamaIndex plays a crucial role in Lyzr's technology stack:

  • Context augmentation: LlamaIndex components supply essential context to Lyzr's agents, creating Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems that enable them to perform focused and effective work.
  • Custom data access: LlamaIndex’s data connectors are the preferred way for Lyzr agents to access customer-specific information. LlamaIndex’s huge library of connectors means they can connect no matter where the customer stores their data.
  • Flexible retrieval: Lyzr uses LlamaIndex's customizable retrieval methods to optimize performance for different use cases. Lyzr’s AutoRAG system determines the optimal retrieval model, chunk size, and other parameters based on input data and use case and passes those to LlamaIndex.
