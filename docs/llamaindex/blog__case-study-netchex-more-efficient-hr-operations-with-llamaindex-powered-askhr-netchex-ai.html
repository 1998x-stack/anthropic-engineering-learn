Netchex is a leader in payroll and human resources software solutions for small to medium sized businesses. Netchex serves over 6,500 clients across the United States concentrated in industries with many hourly desk-less workers such as restaurants, hotels, healthcare, manufacturing, auto dealers and professional services. Their target customers often lack large HR teams, creating a need for efficient, automated HR solutions.

To meet this customer need, Netchex used LlamaIndex to create AskHR + Netchex AI that dramatically improves HR efficiency and employee support.

Netchex's customers faced significant hurdles in their HR operations. HR teams were overwhelmed by a constant influx of employee queries, leading to delayed responses and reduced productivity. Moreover, despite having comprehensive documentation like employee handbooks and benefit plan summaries , companies struggled to efficiently leverage this information to provide quick answers to staff inquiries. The existing solutions were clunky and lacked user-friendly interfaces.

💡 The Solution: AskHR + Netchex AI powered by LlamaIndex

Netchex chose LlamaIndex to power Netchex AI. The implementation used several key features of LlamaIndex: - Document Ingestion and Processing: U...

Netchex chose LlamaIndex to power Netchex AI. The implementation used several key features of LlamaIndex:

  • Document Ingestion and Processing:
Used LlamaIndex's ingestion pipeline to process various document types including MS Office files and PDFs.
  • Employed chunking strategies on large HR documents like employee handbooks and benefit plans. LlamaIndex’s Azure OpenAI integration was used to generate embeddings.
  • Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG):
Implemented advanced RAG pipelines to ensure AI responses were grounded in the company's specific HR documents.
  • Used LlamaIndex's customizable retriever classes to fine-tune the relevance of retrieved information.
  • Employed the flexible query engine architecture to process employee questions and synthesize accurate, context-aware responses.
  • Utilized the citation feature to provide direct links to source documents and page numbers in AI responses.
  • Scalability and Semantic Routing
Took advantage of LlamaIndex's scalable architecture to handle varying loads across Netchex's diverse client base.
  • Leveraged LlamaIndex's router capabilities to implement semantic routing, efficiently directing queries to the most appropriate knowledge bases or response generation pipelines.

Remarkably, this sophisticated system was developed by just two engineers in approximately one month! Just 20% of their time was on LlamaIndex-related work. This speed of implementation was largely due to LlamaIndex's intuitive API and comprehensive documentation, allowing for quick prototyping and deployment.
