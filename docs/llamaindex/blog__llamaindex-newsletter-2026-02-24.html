Welcome to this week&#39;s edition of the LlamaIndex newsletter! We&#39;re excited to bring you updates including major enhancements to LlamaAgents Builder with file upload support, a hands-on Gemini 3.1 Pro receipt analyzer demo, insights on reasoning models vs. document parsing, and powerful new citation capabilities. Check out these developments along with our comprehensive guides and tutorials to maximize your use of these new features.

Over the next few weeks, you’ll see LlamaCloud transition to LlamaParse across our docs and website. Over the past year, our enterprise platform has evolved into a truly agentic document processing platform, with LlamaParse at its core. Renaming the product reflects that evolution and sharpens our focus on what we do best: powerful document parsing and agentic workflows for document-centric automation. More innovations coming soon!
