⚙️ The Solution: LlamaParse

Pursuit partnered with LlamaIndex to leverage LlamaParse, a state-of-the-art document parsing platform built to parse documents for LLM applications. By implementing LlamaParse, Pursuit achieved the f

Scalable Document Parsing: LlamaParse provided Pursuit with the ability to parse millions of pages in a single scalable framework. In one weekend alone, Pursuit was able to parse 4 million pages, making large-scale document ingestion feasible and efficient. Accurate Data Extraction: LlamaParse provides a high level of accuracy even with complex document formats. This allowed Pursuit to extract key pieces of information from tables, figures, scanned images and unstructured content, enabling their clients to filter data by specific parameters. For their datasets, Pursuit saw a 25-30% increase in accuracy scores.

📊 Impact

LlamaParse's accurate and fast extraction had a transformative impact on Pursuit's customers:

Increased Opportunity Discovery: The filtered, searchable database of parsed documents allowed Pursuit's clients to identify potential opportunities that were previously hidden in the unstructured data, such as new public safety initiatives or untapped funding streams. Effective Account Targeting: Leveraging the LlamaParse-powered data, Pursuit provided its clients with targeted insights into the top public safety initiatives across all accounts, enabling more strategic engagement and a higher success rate in winning public sector contracts.
