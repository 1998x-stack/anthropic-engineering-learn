### How to customize your sandbox compute

To customize your sandbox compute, you need to build your custom sandbox template using the [E2B CLI](/docs/cli/installation). Run [`e2b template build`](/docs/cli/commands#template-build) and specify the `--cpu-count` and `--memory-mb` options.

If you want to try the E2B sandboxes for running your AI app or agent, start [here](/docs). Let us know at [hello@e2b.dev](mailto:hello@e2b.dev) if you need any support.
