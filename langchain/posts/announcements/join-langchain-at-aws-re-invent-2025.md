### **See What&#x27;s New**

We&#x27;ll be showing our latest features, such as Insights Agent and Multi-turn Evaluations. Come by and learn how you can run LangSmith on your AWS infrastructure. If you&#x27;re hitting challenges with production agents or trying to figure out evaluation strategies, this is a good place to get technical feedback.

### Meet Harrison

Harrison will be at the booth on December 3rd from 12:00-1:00pm PT. This is a chance to ask questions about roadmap decisions, share what&#x27;s working (or not working) in your implementations, and hear what we&#x27;re learning from teams who have agents in production. Space is limited, so if you want to attend, add it to your calendar.

If you aren&#x27;t able to make it, our engineering team will be at the booth throughout the conference for technical discussions and demos.

[Add it to your calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/event?action=TEMPLATE&amp;tmeid=NTU0OHVnNXRkbGtqbDZ1b2FvdjE4bnQ4NTEgY19jNTAwMmY2ZDk2MzVhOGE1MzhkYzFhZmUxY2M1YTE0ZjQ2NDFkMmE0MDJkNzYwZTU5M2YyNmNkMjMwODMwZmY4QGc&amp;tmsrc=c_c5002f6d9635a8a538dc1afe1cc5a14f4641d2a402d760e593f26cd230830ff8%40group.calendar.google.com&amp;ref=blog.langchain.com)

## **Events Throughout the Week**

We&#x27;re participating in a few technical sessions during the week:

### LangChain x OpenSearch Event

*December 1st | 5:30-8:30pm PT | Encore Hotel*

Our engineering team will cover context engineering patterns we&#x27;ve seen work well with LangGraph and OpenSearch. There will be live demos, a panel discussion, and time to connect with other developers. This event is open to all attendees. [Register now](https://pulse.aws/application/RYGWW6LK?p=0&amp;ref=blog.langchain.com).

### Redis Partner Lightning Session

*December 3rd at 1:15pm | Redis Booth, Expo Floor - Booth #1520*

Our engineering team will co-present with Redis a 10-minute lightning talk on the basics of context engineering and demo a deep-research AI agent built with LangChain and Redis.

### AI LIVE at re:Invent with LangChain

*December 2nd at 12:33-12:53 PM PT | The Venetian Expo Floor – Level 2, Hall B, Partner Experience Pavilion #884 | Booth - Partners LIVE! #1190*

Hear from our team live on the expo floor! We’ll be discussing the evolution from simple LLM applications to full-fledged agentic systems — covering context engineering, deep agents, and the agent development lifecycle.

[Add to calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/event?action=TEMPLATE&amp;tmeid=N2ZmMmM3bGUxc3VjNzI2ZXZuNTR0c3F1NG8gY19jNTAwMmY2ZDk2MzVhOGE1MzhkYzFhZmUxY2M1YTE0ZjQ2NDFkMmE0MDJkNzYwZTU5M2YyNmNkMjMwODMwZmY4QGc&amp;tmsrc=c_c5002f6d9635a8a538dc1afe1cc5a14f4641d2a402d760e593f26cd230830ff8%40group.calendar.google.com&amp;ref=blog.langchain.com)

## Self-Host LangSmith on AWS

LangSmith is now available on AWS Marketplace. You can host LangSmith on your own AWS infrastructure and bill against your AWS commit. If you&#x27;re exploring self-hosted options, we&#x27;ll have live demos at Booth #524. Check out LangSmith on [AWS Marketplace](https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/pp/prodview-vmzygmggk4gms?ref=blog.langchain.com).

## Let&#x27;s Connect

If you want to dig into specific challenges you&#x27;re facing—whether that&#x27;s evaluation strategies, deployment patterns, or scaling multi-agent systems—our team is available to meet during re:Invent. Request a meeting and let us know what you&#x27;d like to discuss.

Can&#x27;t make it to re:Invent? We&#x27;re happy to connect afterward.

## See You in Vegas

Whether you&#x27;re evaluating tools, debugging production issues, or exploring new patterns— stop by. We&#x27;ll have our engineering team available for technical conversations about what you&#x27;re building.
