 We’ve been delighted with the adoption of [LlamaParse](https://www.llamaindex.ai/enterprise), our turn-key solution for enterprises looking to build fast, accurate knowledge management systems across their unstructured data. Its easy connection with hundreds of data sources and seamless integration into the larger agentic LlamaIndex open source framework has enabled companies like KPMG, Cemex, Carlyle and Salesforce to build secure and capable knowledge-management applications. Enterprise developers are able to ship use cases in days vs months with LlamaParse!



 Compliance is a paramount concern when dealing with enterprise unstructured data, and for customers based in Europe data residency has been a barrier to adoption. To address that, we are happy to announce the early availability of [LlamaParse EU](https://cloud.eu.llamaindex.ai/). Our new EU-based SaaS offering ensures data residency entirely to EU jurisdiction when processing your business-critical data.
