 Today we’re delighted to announce two milestones: the general availability of LlamaParse, and our $19M series A funding round.







 LlamaParse is the core of our enterprise offering: a turn-key solution for agentic knowledge management over your unstructured data. It’s the most secure, accurate, and easy to use platform to enable building knowledge agents. Use one of our hundreds of available integrations to connect to your enterprise data, and let us take care of parsing, ingesting, indexing, and retrieval for you, as well as uptime and reliability. LlamaParse is available both as an instant-setup SaaS offering and an on-premise solution for higher security and data locality guarantees. It’s also highly private and secure, with SOC 2 Type 2 certification. Now in general availability, you can [sign up today](https://cloud.llamaindex.ai/) and join happy customers like Rakuten, Carlyle, Salesforce and KPMG. If you’re in the EU, you may be interested in our GDPR-compliant [EU SaaS offering](https://www.llamaindex.ai/blog/announcing-early-access-to-llamacloud-eu), featuring guaranteed EU data residency.

 LlamaParse is powered by LlamaParse, our industry-leading solution for parsing complex, unstructured document formats like PDFs, Word documents, PowerPoints, and hundreds of other formats. LlamaParse takes care of complex elements like tables, charts and images with ease, and has handled hundreds of millions of documents for tens of thousands of satisfied users already. [Learn more about LlamaParse](https://www.llamaindex.ai/llamaparse).







 You can build on top of LlamaParse and LlamaParse using the LlamaIndex framework, our leading open-source framework in [Python](https://docs.llamaindex.ai/en/stable/) and [TypeScript](https://ts.llamaindex.ai/). We’ve got more than 3 million monthly downloads, almost 40,000 GitHub stars, and hundreds of thousands of active developers worldwide. Our event-driven [Workflows](https://docs.llamaindex.ai/en/stable/understanding/workflows/) architecture makes it easy to put together scalable, predictable AI agents, and developers love it!







 Our series A is led by Norwest Venture Partners, with participation from our existing investors Greylock Partners, bringing our total capital raised to $27.5M. Dave Zilberman, general partner at Norwest Venture Partners, says “LlamaIndex is compelling for Norwest as we’ve been closely studying enterprise requirements for AI adoption, specifically around data preparation, ingestion and custom agents as part of an ongoing investment thesis. The founding team has demonstrated grit and resilience, building usable, enterprise-enabling capabilities to solve critical pain points—from production accuracy issues to scaling complex data workflows. With customers already lined up, LlamaIndex has the potential to empower the agentic enterprise. We’re excited to see where the next year takes them.&quot;







 With our new injection of funding, we’re going to be expanding our team! We’re hiring in all sorts of positions to expand our OSS frameworks, further improve LlamaParse, go even deeper on LlamaParse, and accelerate our go-to-market. Check out our [open positions](https://www.llamaindex.ai/careers)!
