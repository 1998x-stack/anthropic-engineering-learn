##  Event Highlights


-  Over 500 registrations

 The hackathon challenged participants to go beyond basic chatbots, encouraging the development of agentic RAG systems. We were particularly interested to see people build [agents](https://docs.llamaindex.ai/en/stable/understanding/agent/basic_agent/) and combine them with our [workflows](https://docs.llamaindex.ai/en/stable/understanding/workflows/) to create multi-agent systems, and we were not disappointed! ##  Winning Projects



###  1st Place: [Timeline of You](https://devpost.com/software/timeline-of-you)

  ![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/7m9jw85w/production/1f5ca7a559c219a02ff9cea58db29bc4ac499310-1426x1070.webp) The Timeline of You team

 The grand prize winner, &quot;Timeline of You,&quot; impressed judges with its comprehensive approach to personal data analysis. This innovative project:


-  Ingests various forms of personal data, including resumes, LinkedIn profiles, and academic citations
  -  Generates a detailed timeline of career accomplishments
  -  Creates tailored content, such as O1 visa applications with appropriate reference letters
  -  Provides engaging visualizations of personal data



 The team&#39;s exceptional user interface and clear market potential set this project apart, exemplifying the power of RAG-informed report generation.



###  2nd Place: [Closing.WTF](https://devpost.com/software/closing-wtf)

  ![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/7m9jw85w/production/462ca704d72f8ceb536eb2a8831d167412f5353f-802x1070.webp) 2nd-place winner Aaron (and his delightful family!)

 Taking the runner-up spot, &quot;Closing.WTF&quot; addresses a common pain point in the real estate market:


-  Analyzes mortgage closing documents using LlamaParse
  -  Identifies potential areas for negotiation and flags anomalies
  -  Presents findings through an intuitive user interface



 This practical application of RAG-informed report generation impressed judges with its clear use-case and significant market potential.



###  3rd Place: [OilyRAGs](https://devpost.com/software/oilyrags)

  ![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/7m9jw85w/production/a605fe6c0e10d38d42f31860299e6422d1dfd8d6-802x1070.webp) 3rd place winner Jeff

 Securing third place, &quot;OilyRAGs&quot; demonstrated the value of AI in specialized industries:


-  Developed an automation tool for boat mechanics
  -  Indexes part catalogs and service manuals
  -  Generates reports on related parts and required services based on part numbers
  -  Includes a mobile interface with multimodal recognition for part number identification



 Despite facing technical challenges during development, Jeff delivered a robust solution that showcases the practical applications of report generation in streamlining existing processes.



##  Special Prize Winners



 In addition to our grand prizes, several projects earned recognition from our sponsors with specific prizes:


-  **[ToolHouse.ai](https://toolhouse.ai/) Prize**: [Gift Genie](https://devpost.com/software/giftgenie-gift-suggestion-app) - An AI-powered gift suggestion app that analyzes social media profiles to recommend personalized gifts.
  -  **[VESSL AI](https://vessl.ai/) Award**: [SteamScape AI](https://devpost.com/software/immerse-into-real-steam-games) - A sophisticated game recommendation system utilizing an existing Steam games database.
  -  **[500 Global](https://500.co/) Award**: [RAG Context Refinement Agent](https://devpost.com/software/rag-context-refinement-agent) - An advanced implementation of RAG techniques to improve context retrieval and filtering.
  -  **[Reflex](https://reflex.dev/) Award**: [Pure ML](https://devpost.com/software/pureml) - A tool for ingesting and enriching ML datasets with synthetic data.
  -  **[Box](https://www.box.com/) Award**: [RAGFormation](https://devpost.com/software/ragformation) - An ambitious project generating cloud architecture suggestions, system diagrams, and cost estimates.
  -  **[Arize Phoenix](https://phoenix.arize.com/) Award**: [OpsRocket](https://devpost.com/software/pinnacle-strategies) - An AI consulting tool aimed at improving business operations.
